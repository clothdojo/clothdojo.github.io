Protocol & interpretation

All configurations start from pretrained π₀.₅ and train for three epochs on their respective datasets. White uses uniform garment appearance; subsequent rows cumulatively add canonical surface colors, mirror augmentation, and human corrections. Execution limits are 60 s for flattening and 40 s for folding.

At first confirmed success, the full configuration has mean deformation / orientation errors of 0.080 / 0.024, versus 0.068 / 0.019 for expert references (lower is better). Folding quality Q is 0.825, versus 1.037 for expert references (higher is better). Metrics use different successful subsets; higher success does not imply uniformly better terminal quality.

Training & evaluation protocol

Each condition uses 100 garments and four fixed initial states. All policies start from the same pretrained π₀.₅ weights and train for three epochs. Human and Generated use the same re-rendering procedure; Combined pools both. Seen garments appear in all three training sets, and unseen garments in none.

Combined does not improve on Generated alone. The cause remains unverified. Equal epochs on differently sized datasets do not imply equal training steps.

What this comparison controls

All models share the reported data source and evaluation conditions. π₀.₅, GR00T N1.7 and the internal StarVLA-based policy are fine-tuned; Diffusion Policy is trained from scratch. Pretraining differences are included, so this is not a controlled architecture-only ablation. StarVLA* denotes an internal policy implemented with the public framework.

How to read the examples

Percentiles are ranked within each source’s eligible subset, not paired trials of the same garment. The images illustrate the quality range rather than a matched visual comparison.
